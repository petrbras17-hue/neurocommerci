# Easy AI -- Neurocommenting Service

**URL:** https://ai-easy.ru/
**Type:** SaaS Service (Direct Competitor)
**Scraped:** 2026-03-23

## Summary

Easy AI is a Russian neurocommenting service focused on Telegram channel promotion. They use neural networks to leave comments on posts in thematic channels, simulating human behavior. Contact: @usernamewwws on Telegram.

## How It Works (Their Description)

"With the help of accounts, our software leaves comments on posts of thematic channels, doing it like a real person, thanks to the neural network."

Key workflow:
1. Software monitors target channels for new posts
2. Neural network analyzes post content
3. AI generates a relevant, human-like comment
4. Comment is posted from the user's account
5. Profile packaging (avatar + name + bio with offer) attracts attention
6. Users click through to your channel/bot/group

## Traffic Flow

Profile packaging is critical:
- Bright avatar
- "Speaking" name (with offer)
- Bio with clear CTA
- Link to target channel/bot/chat

People see the comment -> click profile -> follow link -> land on your channel

## Performance Claims

- One Telegram account can comment on up to 1,000 channels
- Bot writes ~3,500 live comments per day from one account
- Comments appear instantly after post publication
- Subscriber cost: from 3 RUB (vs 100+ RUB in targeted ads)

## Key Takeaways

1. **3,500 comments/day/account** -- very aggressive rate; questions about ban risk
2. **1,000 channels per account** -- ambitious target
3. **Instant commenting after post** -- speed is a competitive advantage
4. **Profile packaging is critical** -- avatar, name, bio optimization matters as much as comments
5. **Cost per subscriber: 3 RUB** -- main selling point vs paid advertising
6. **Neural network = ChatGPT-based** -- standard approach in the industry

## Anti-Ban Considerations (Not Explicitly Mentioned)

Their aggressive rates (3,500/day) suggest either:
- Very good anti-ban tech (account warming, delays, proxy rotation)
- Short account lifespan with high replacement rate
- OR inflated marketing numbers

## Competitive Analysis

- They focus purely on commenting (not full combine like GramGPT)
- Claim very high volume (3,500/day) which is aggressive
- No visible pricing on website -- consultation-based sales
- No mention of multi-account management or health monitoring
