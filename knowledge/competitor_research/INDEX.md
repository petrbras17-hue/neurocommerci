## Key Market Findings

### Pricing Landscape
| Service | Model | Price Range |
|---------|-------|-------------|
| GramGPT | Modular SaaS | $40/mo (commenting) - $130/mo (full) |
| AiCommenting | Subscription | 5,500-12,500 RUB/30-90 days |
| Easy AI | Consultation | Not public |
| NeuroCom | Consultation | Not public |
| TgNinja | Tiered SaaS | "Guru" tier+ |
| Open source | Free | $0 (but no support/anti-ban) |

### Performance Benchmarks
- **Comments/day/account:** up to 3,500 (Easy AI claim, likely aggressive)
- **Channels per account:** 160 (conservative) to 1,000 (aggressive)
- **Cost per subscriber:** 3-30 RUB (vs 100-500 RUB via targeted ads)
- **Subscriber growth:** 10-30/day typical, up to 450/month in best cases
- **Comment delay:** 10-12 seconds recommended to avoid detection
- **Account scale:** 5-20 accounts per setup is standard

### Anti-Ban Best Practices (Consolidated)
1. **Comment delay:** 10-12 seconds minimum after post publication
2. **Quarantine on flood wait:** 24 hours even for 60-second restrictions
3. **Proxy diversity:** Mobile for new accounts, residential for stability, datacenter for scale
4. **Account warming:** Gradual activity increase over days/weeks
5. **Profile variety:** Avoid the "successful person" stereotype; vary avatars, names, tones
6. **Comment variety:** Different prompts, tones (friendly/expert/provocative), lengths
7. **Channel rotation:** Don't comment in the same channels repeatedly from one account
8. **Rest periods:** Allow accounts to rest periodically

### Detection Vectors to Avoid
1. Commenting too fast (seconds after post)
2. Consistent writing style across accounts
3. Overly positive/supportive tone in every comment
4. "Sales-y" profile aesthetics (money symbols, success promises)
5. Repetitive phrase patterns
6. Same IP across multiple accounts

### Customer Segments
1. **CPA/Affiliate Marketers** -- need traffic sources (Partnerkin audience)
2. **Business Owners/Experts** -- need subscribers and leads (Spark.ru audience)
3. **Channel Admins** -- need channel growth (aicommenting audience)
4. **Crypto/Finance** -- highest-performing niche
5. **Marketing Agencies** -- need scalable solutions (managed service tier)
