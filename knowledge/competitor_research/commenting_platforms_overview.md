# Neurocommenting Platforms & Services -- Complete Overview

Last updated: 2026-03-23

## Market Overview

Neurocommenting (AI-powered automated commenting in Telegram) is a rapidly growing market segment, primarily driven by Russian-speaking Telegram marketing community. According to Forbes.ru, approximately 12% of all comments in Russian Telegram channels with 100K+ subscribers are generated by neuro-commenting bots. The market has matured significantly since 2023-2024, with multiple commercial products and open-source tools now available.

## Key Market Insights

1. **Price range**: $50-130/month for self-service; $25/hr for custom development
2. **Sweet spot**: ~5,000 RUB/month (~$55) for 3 accounts, 160 channels each
3. **Volume**: Top performers claim 3,500 comments/account/day across 1,000 channels
4. **Tech stack**: Almost all use ChatGPT API for comment generation
5. **Client base**: Primarily Russian-speaking market, some international expansion
6. **Delivery model**: Split between Telegram bot management and web-based dashboards
7. **Account management**: Most require clients to provide their own Telegram accounts and proxies
8. **Anti-detection**: Human-like delays, proxy rotation, account warming are standard features
9. **Forbes validation**: The fact that Forbes.ru covered this phenomenon signals mainstream awareness
10. **Growth**: 3 accounts can generate 30-60 new subscribers/day (900-1,800/month)
