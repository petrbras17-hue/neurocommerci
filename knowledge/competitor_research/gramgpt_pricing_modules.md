## Pricing Analysis

### Cost Comparison: Individual Modules vs Full License

**Scenario: Neuro Commenting + Neuro Chatting + Neuro Dialogs (core AI modules)**
- Individual monthly: $40 + $40 + $35 = $115/month
- Full license monthly: $130/month (all modules)
- Full license yearly: $60/month equivalent

**Breakeven:** If you need 3+ premium modules, Full License is cheaper.

### Price Tiers:
- **Premium tier ($35-40/month):** Neuro Commenting, Neuro Chatting, Neuro Dialogs
- **Mid tier ($25/month):** Mass React
- **Budget tier ($8/month):** All parsers (Comment, Message, User, Group, Channel)
- **Free:** Telegram Map
- **Included:** Account Manager, AI Protection, Account Warming

### Yearly vs Monthly:
- All modules offer exactly 42% discount for yearly plans
- Yearly price = Monthly * 7 (so you pay for 7 months, get 12)

1. **Modular upsell** — start with cheap parser ($8), upgrade to AI modules ($40)
2. **Lock-in via full license** — yearly at $720 is hard to leave
3. **Free Telegram Map** — lead generation tool (try free, then buy modules)
4. **"16 modules" marketing** — counts parsers individually to inflate number
5. **Support as value-add** — 24/7 support emphasized as differentiator
6. **Video tutorials** — each module has dedicated YouTube video for onboarding
