## Summary

Comprehensive article from MoreLogin (anti-detect browser vendor) covering three main Telegram promotion methods: neuro-commenting, masslooking, and inviting. Written from the perspective of a tool vendor serving automation users.

## Key Insights on Neuro-Commenting

### How it works:
- Network of prepared accounts operating via proxies and anti-detect browsers
- Accounts follow channels with active target audiences
- Monitor new posts and immediately post natural, well-crafted comments
- Creates the effect of live discussion
- Attracts high-intent users without direct advertising

### Best niches:
- Finance, crypto, traffic arbitrage, media, entertainment
- Discussion-driven communities where users read comments and click profiles
- Active Telegram communities with moderate content competition

### Risks identified:
- Telegram actively combats unnatural activity and spam
- Excessive repetitive messages lead to bans
- Poorly contextualized comments reduce trust and attract low-quality traffic

### Requirements for effective execution:
1. Contextual relevance
2. Natural wording
3. Limited account volumes
4. Controlled posting frequency
5. Analytics monitoring (CTR, engagement, retention)

## Masslooking (Alternative Method)

- Systematically viewing stories at scale
- Sending reactions to stories
- Creating mentions in stories/posts
- Triggers curiosity -> users visit target profile/channel
- Risk: shallow traffic with weak retention
- Best for fast reach expansion and brand visibility

## Inviting (Alternative Method)

- Direct method through personalized invitations
- Invitations from related Telegram groups
- Partnerships with thematic communities
- Special links with bonuses/exclusive content
- Events (webinars, AMA sessions, contests)
- Higher conversion but risk of "dead audience" (subscribe for bonus only)
