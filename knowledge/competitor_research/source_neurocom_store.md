**Type:** SaaS Platform (Direct Competitor -- Multi-Platform)

## Summary

NeuroCom.store is a multi-platform neurocommenting service that covers Telegram, Instagram, and VK. They position themselves as a complete lead generation system using AI-powered commenting. They also have a Dzen blog and are registered as a legal entity in Russia.

## Key Differentiator: Multi-Platform

Unlike most competitors, NeuroCom covers:
- **Telegram** (primary)
- **Instagram** (Meta Platforms marked as extremist in RF)
- **VK** (Russian social network)

## How It Works (Telegram)

Every time a post goes out in one of the target channels:
1. One of your 5 accounts leaves a small attention-grabbing comment
2. The neural network writes comments instantly after the post
3. Comments are context-aware (based on post content)
4. Profile packaging drives traffic to your channel/bot
