## Case Study 1: +460 Subscribers in 45 Days

**Author:** Anton ITshnik (same person who runs aicommenting.tb.ru)

### Strategy:

- Post in thematic channels where audience is already warmed up

## Teletype Case Studies (from @pathdream / Fire Jump)

### Step-by-Step Implementation System:
1. Package the expert -- photo, name, compelling phrases in title
2. Set up minimum 5-20 accounts for neurocommenting
3. Build channel database -- minimum 500-2000 channels with comments enabled
4. Launch the automatic neurocommenting process

### Real Results from Cases:
- **Crypto expert:** Paid 5,800 RUB, got 248 subscribers in 10 days = 23 RUB/subscriber. Used 200 channels.
- **Astrology:** 110 subscribers in 11 days
- **Expert channels:** 130 subscribers in bot, 83 in channel, cost 26 RUB/subscriber
- **Consulting:** 88 subscribers, 5 consultations, 1 sale for 200,000 RUB
- **Marketing expert:** 450 subscribers in first month, 4 sales at 35K, 1 sale at 300K
- **Marketing lead magnet:** 20% conversion to lead magnet, 54 people in bot

### Key Metrics:
- **Speed tip:** Set comment delay to 10-12 seconds so channel admin doesn't notice it's a bot
- **Cost range:** 3-30 RUB per subscriber (vs 100-500 RUB for targeted ads)
- **Best niches:** Crypto, consulting, expert content, astrology, marketing

## Key Takeaways

1. **Comment delay of 10-12 seconds** is recommended to avoid detection
2. **5-20 accounts per setup** is standard operational scale
3. **500-2000 channels** in the target database is optimal
4. **Cost per subscriber: 3-30 RUB** -- main economic advantage
5. **Profile = landing page** -- this is where conversion happens
6. **First subscribers come within days** -- 30/day in first 3 days in some cases
7. **Sales come within first weeks** -- not just subscribers but actual revenue
8. **Channel selection matters most** -- thematic relevance drives quality
